## Design Specificity Verdict
- **LLM Assessment**: High specificity. Grounded in strength training workflows, 1-thumb gym ergonomics, and high-contrast ambient readability.
- **Deterministic Scan**: 0 AI slop tells, 0 errors, 2 minor layout animation warnings (animating max-height), 31 token advisories.

## Priority Issues
- **[P1] Sub-44px Touch Targets on Secondary Actions**: Secondary action buttons (Edit, Rename, Delete, Cardio Add) have min-height 38px in CustomView and HistoryView.
- **[P2] CSS Transition Performance in CustomView**: .rename-slide-enter-active animates max-height causing reflow risks on low-end mobile devices.
- **[P3] No In-Builder Exercise Reordering**: Reordering exercises in Day Builder requires manual recreation.
- **[P3] External Video Navigation**: Demo links open external YouTube search tabs rather than an embedded sheet.

## Persona Red Flags
- **Casey (Distracted Trainee at Gym)**: 38px secondary buttons pose mis-tap risk with sweaty hands.
- **Alex (Trainer / Power User)**: Cannot drag-to-reorder exercises in Day Builder.
- **Jordan (First-Time Trainee)**: External YouTube link breaks workout session momentum.
