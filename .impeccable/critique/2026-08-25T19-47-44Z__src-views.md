#### Overall Impression

The Workout App is structurally solid and functionally tuned for real gym use, but suffers from **three visual friction points**: low contrast under bright gym lights, tiny touch targets for sweaty hands mid-workout, and page overcrowding in `CustomView`. Addressing these will transform it from a functional tool into a seamless, high-craft mobile PWA.

#### What's Working

1. **Flawless Domain Alignment**: Terminology, phase structure (Foundation/Build/Strength), track toggles (Home/Gym), and set/rep/weight/cardio mechanics directly match real-world training rituals.
2. **Offline-First Resilience**: Real-time connection indicators (`● Offline`, `↻ Syncing…`) and IndexedDB queued logging ensure the trainer is never stranded mid-session without connectivity.
3. **Smart Modal Logging**: Pre-populating target reps as placeholders in the set-logging modal reduces cognitive load and manual typing during workouts.

#### Priority Issues

- **[P1] Low Contrast Under Gym Lighting**
  - **Why it matters**: Subtext and labels using `#555`, `#444`, and `#666` on `oklch(8-10%)` dark backgrounds yield contrast ratios as low as ~2.4:1 (below WCAG AA 4.5:1), making screens unreadable under bright gym overhead lights or sun glare.
  - **Fix**: Elevate muted text colors to `#888` / `#aaa` or `oklch(70% 0.005 45)` minimum across all views.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable colorize`

- **[P1] Sub-Optimal Touch Targets for One-Handed Gym Use**
  - **Why it matters**: Pipe-separated inline text buttons (`Edit | Activate | Rename | Delete`) and text action links have ~20px heights, leading to frequent miss-taps when holding a phone between heavy sets.
  - **Fix**: Convert inline text actions into discrete pill/chip buttons with minimum 44×44px hit areas and proper padding.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable adapt`

- **[P2] Visual Overcrowding on CustomView**
  - **Why it matters**: `CustomView.vue` stacks three complex workflows (*My Programs*, *Quick Day Override*, *Saved Custom Days*) on one long page, creating visual noise and confusing competing actions.
  - **Fix**: Reorganize `CustomView` with clean tabbed navigation ("Programs" vs "Day Overrides") or distinct collapsible sections.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

- **[P2] `side-tab` Accent Border Anti-Pattern**
  - **Why it matters**: 3px and 4px left border stripes on dark cards (`HistoryView` & `ProgramView`) create a recognized AI-generated layout trope.
  - **Fix**: Replace left border stripes with subtle inline status pills or 8px indicator dots.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`
